# Google Search Console

The project already includes:
- `/robots.txt`
- `/sitemap.xml`
- `google-site-verification.html` as a placeholder

For the final verification, open Google Search Console and choose the HTML-file method.
Google will provide a unique filename/token. Replace the placeholder `public/google-site-verification.html`
with the exact file Google gives you (including the exact filename/content), deploy, and click Verify.

Then submit:
https://starbridge.finance/sitemap.xml
